Arkham Party Cards

Paste your party's Arkham Horror: The Card Game deck links from ArkhamDB, and get a combined shopping list of how many copies of each card the group needs.

Only public decklists work (arkhamdb.com/decklist/view/...). Private "My Decks" links (arkhamdb.com/deck/view/...) need a login ArkhamDB won't allow from another website, so those will fail — publish the deck (there's a "Publish" button on the deck page) to get a link that works here.
